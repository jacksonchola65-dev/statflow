# Level-5 Decision Workspace Demo

Estimated duration: 3-5 minutes.

1. Open `/decisions` while authenticated.
2. Enter `Where should I open a supermarket in Luapula?` in the natural-language field.
3. Select **Interpret and analyze**. Show the `SUPPORTED` interpretation, Business Location model, supermarket, Luapula, district geography, and production mode.
4. Point out the production result: `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, 25% Evidence Readiness, and no recommendation.
5. Walk through the six-criterion portfolio. Market Demand is production usable; the other five criteria are blocked. Open the blocker and evidence-backlog sections.
6. Switch to **EXPLORATORY** mode and explicitly select **Analyze location opportunity**.
7. Show the safety banner: `Not a production recommendation.` Review the Market Demand ranking, with Mansa as the Market Demand Leader and 12 districts listed.
8. Select Mansa to inspect the provenance: POP_TOTAL, 2022, People, 329,622, ZamStats, dataset reference, freshness, and the honest `unknown` quality state.
9. Return to Production mode and emphasize that the exploratory leader does not populate the production recommendation.
10. Explain that the missing district-compatible evidence for growth, purchasing power, accessibility, competition, and operating feasibility is what must be supplied before a production recommendation can be issued.

The demonstration describes evidence and model readiness. It does not claim that Mansa is the best business location.
